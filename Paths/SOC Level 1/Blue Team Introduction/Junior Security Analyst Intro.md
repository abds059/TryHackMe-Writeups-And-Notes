# Junior Security Analyst Intro

## Task 1 - Junior Security Analyst Journey

### Security Analyst Journey

In the cybersecurity landscape every new threat can destroy a business and every click may hide an attack. This is why there is an ever increasing need of a large number of defenders to stop these type of attacks and keep companies and data safe.

### Your Daily Duties

A Junior security analyst is a one that identifies and analyses attacks, stop data breaches and keep companies safe. As a junior analyst, also called SOC Level 1 Analyst, you work in a 24/7 SOC team and reviews alerts together with your colleagues.

The work shift involves work like:

- Monitoring and investigating various security alerts
- Participating in SOC brainstorms and workshops
- Cooperating with other teams to keep your company safe
- Constantly learning and discovering new attacks and defenses

## Task 2 - Security Operations Center (SOC)

### SOC and Our Team

We are not alone in monitoring and mitigating alerts. In SOC a lot of other people are involved in securing the company as well. SOC engineers configure tools, Senior Analyst help with complex tasks and SOC manager keeps everything under control. 

For this room we have been assigned the following team mates:

- #### Will Griffin - Senior Analyst

    Will is your closest colleague. He helps you and other Junior analysts when something is unclear and handles complex cases after you do the initial analysis.

- #### Corey Stevens - SOC Engineer
    
    Corey doesn't have shifts and doesn't analyze the alerts. Instead, he maintains security tools and configures the alerts to make your analyst's job easier.

- #### Emily Conway - SOC Manager

    Emily tries to keep everything under control. She reports SOC results to the top management and makes sure you aren't lost in that big new cyber security world.

- #### Daniel Herrera- Incident Responder

    You don't work with Daniel every day, but when he's online, you know something serious has happened. He is called on demand during major incidents.

### Daily Duties

Cybersecurity is a broad field and there is always a chanche to grow and gain more expertise in plenty of other roles and with time we can disocver the path that excites us the most. But before that a work experience as Junior Security Analyst is a need as it provides us with an opportunity to gain valuable lessons and face challenges like:

- Detecting and preventing a data stealer infection on a coworker's laptop
- Analyzing and stoping a phishing campaign targeting the finance team
- Participating in a bigger incident, such as a full-scale ransomware attack
- Teaming up with teammates to build detection rules and automations
- Going beyond cyber and understand how companies operate from the inside
